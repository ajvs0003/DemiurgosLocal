Project Brief: Proyecto Nae (The Digital Grimoire)

Project Overview

Proyecto Nae is a high-fidelity digital interface designed for tabletop roleplaying gamers (DMs and players). It blends the immersive aesthetic of a medieval grimoire with modern digital functionality to manage campaigns, rules, and world-building lore.

Design Vision

The interface simulates an open, physical book resting on a dark study table. Every digital interaction is grounded in the metaphor of a manuscript, utilizing high-contrast parchment, hand-inked illustrations, and calligraphic typography.

Core Principles

Atmospheric Immersion: Every screen must feel like a physical artifact from the world of Nae.

Technical Clarity: Despite the historical aesthetic, technical gameplay data (spell stats, rules) must be highly legible and organized.

Unified Navigation: A persistent sidebar and header architecture provides quick access to the library's various wings.

Visual Language

Background: Solid dark warm black (#1a1510) representing the scholar's desk.

Surface: Solid light parchment (#F5ECD7) for maximum readability.

Typography:

Titles: Elegant Serif (Newsreader, Georgia) in Crimson or Dark Ink.

Body: Classic Serif (EB Garamond) for a manuscript feel.

Accents: Ancient Gold (#C9A84C) for ornaments and active states; Wine Red (#8B1A1A) for hierarchy and CTA elements.

Feature Modules

The Archive (Grimoire): A categorized database of spells, rules, and items presented in illuminated grid or detail views.

The World: A campaign ledger for tracking active chronicles, featuring high-fidelity landscape mockups and session metadata.

The Sanctum: A personalized workspace for "Masters of Destiny" to draft new entries and manage world-building lore.

Search (The Great Index): A functional search interface using dotted leaders and manuscript-style results.

Technical Requirements

Responsive Layout: Desktop follows a dual-page or single-column parchment spread; Mobile collapses into a single vertical scroll.

Full-Height Sidebar: Persistent left-hand navigation with "The Great Archive" branding block.

Language: Primary interface in Spanish ("Códice", "Maestros del Destino"), supporting multi-language content (English spell text).
